## About me

I’m passionate about working at the intersection of technology, data, and user behavior — not just building systems, but understanding why they matter. Through coursework in data structures, algorithms, and applied statistics, I’ve built a strong technical foundation while staying focused on product strategy and real-world impact.

I’ve applied this mindset in both technical and strategic roles. As a Machine Learning Intern, I built Random Forest and ANN models in Python that achieved up to R² = 0.894 and predicted outcomes within 5–9% accuracy. As I work toward graduating in May 2028, I’m excited to pursue opportunities in software engineering, data analytics, or product management where I can combine technical depth, analytical thinking, and creativity to build user-focused solutions.
